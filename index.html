Questions

Quick answers before you buy.

When does Wreck-It World open?

Opening day is shown in the countdown at the top of this page. Tickets are on sale now for opening year.

Can I bring food and drinks?

Small snacks and sealed water bottles are fine. Coolers and glass are not allowed.

Are there height requirements?

Each ride lists its minimum height. Hi-Score Speedway and Demolition Drop need 44 inches. Most family rides have no minimum.

What if it rains?

San Diego rain is rare, and most rides keep running. If we close for weather, your ticket is good for another date.

Can I change my ticket date?

Yes. Change your date free up to 24 hours before your visit.
